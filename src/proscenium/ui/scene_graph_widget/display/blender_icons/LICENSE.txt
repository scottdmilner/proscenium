row_icon_masks.npz holds coverage masks rasterized from Blender's icons (release/datafiles/icons_svg in Blender
5.2's source, https://projects.blender.org/blender/blender), which the scene graph widget recolors for instances and
their instance proxies.

Blender's icons are by Andrzej Ambroż (jendrzych) and Blender's contributors, and are licensed under the Creative
Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License, as stated at
https://forum.blender.org/t/new-icons-for-blender-2-8x/4651. To view a copy of the license, visit
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/.

Changes: each icon is rasterized to 64 by 64 pixels, its color dropped to keep only how much of each pixel it covers,
and drawn by the widget in theme colors. These masks are licensed under the same license.

The rest of this extension is licensed separately; see its blender_manifest.toml.
